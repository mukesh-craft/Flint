# Task 5 — Loops and math
Write a Flint program in `solution.fl` (with `fn main`) that prints exactly:
- fib(20) (fib(0)=0, fib(1)=1)
- 2 to the power 10 (loop, no pow() call)
- the sum of squares 1..10

Expected output is exactly the three lines in expected.txt.
Ideas you'll need: `fn` recursion, `while`, `for`, compound assignment.
