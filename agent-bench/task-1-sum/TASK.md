# Task 1 — Sum and product
Write a Flint program in `solution.fl` (with `fn main`) that prints exactly:
- the sum of integers 1..100
- the product of integers 1..6
- `1` if the sum is greater than the product, else `0`

Expected output ends with those three lines (each on its own line).
Ideas you'll need: `for i in range(...)`, `mut`, `if/else`, `print`.
